# Gemini Generation Prompt — Purrfect Universe Emoji #001 (:js:)

**Prompt Title:** Purrfect Universe Emoji – JavaScript

Create a **flat, vector emoji-style icon** representing **JavaScript** in the
**Purrfect Universe** visual language.

---

## Concept & Mood

JavaScript is alive, expressive, and confidently imperfect — a crafted tool that
thrives on flexibility, not rigidity. This emoji should capture that energy:
**dynamic yet stable**, **human yet precise**.

---

## Visual Style

- **Overall Form:** Use a **slightly irregular quadrilateral** — for example, a
  **softly tilted square** or a **rhombus with near-parallel sides**. The tilt
  should be **gentle (under 10°)**. It must **still feel like a logo block**,
  not an abstract shape.

- **Base Color:** Flat **JavaScript yellow** (`#f7df1e`).

- **Symbol:** Bold, clean **“JS”** in black. May follow the tilt of the shape or
  sit upright in quiet contrast. Avoid outlines, gradients, or stylistic
  distortion.

- **Feline Accent (Signature Flair):** Add one subtle element hinting at the
  Purrfect Universe origin:
  - a tiny **paw-mark**, or
  - a minimal **tail curve** hugging a corner. This detail must be
    **monochrome** and **barely visible** — like a watermark.

- **Accents (optional, very subtle):** Purrfect Universe tones:
  - `#d2691e` — warmth
  - `#228b22` — growth
  - `#1f77b4` — depth Use sparingly; they should not overpower the yellow.

- **Style Guidelines:**
  - Flat vector only
  - No shadows, bevels, glows, or gradients
  - Clean edges, slight asymmetry permitted
  - Keep the icon readable at small sizes

---

## Background (Chroma Key)

Render the design on a **pure solid background color**: `#00FF43`. This color is
**reserved** for transparency processing — do **not** use it anywhere else in
the emoji.

---

## Output Expectations

- Output a single **PNG** on `#00FF43` background.
- Keep the canvas square and centered.
- Leave slight padding around the shape.
