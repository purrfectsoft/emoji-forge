# Gemini Generation Prompt — Purrfect Universe Emoji #002 (:ts:)

**Prompt Title:** Purrfect Universe Emoji – TypeScript

Create a **flat, vector emoji-style icon** representing **TypeScript** in the
**Purrfect Universe** visual language.

---

## Concept & Mood

TypeScript is calm, deliberate structure layered over creative energy. This
emoji should express **clarity and confidence**: disciplined edges, friendly
tone.

---

## Visual Style

- **Overall Form:** Use a **slightly irregular quadrilateral** — e.g., a
  **softly tilted square** or a **rhombus with near-parallel sides**. The tilt
  should be **gentle (under 10°)**. It must **still feel like a logo block**.

- **Base Color:** Flat **TypeScript blue** (`#3178C6`).

- **Symbol:** Bold, clean **“TS”** in **white**. May follow the tilt or sit
  upright in quiet contrast. Avoid outlines, gradients, or distortion.

- **Feline Accent (Signature Flair):** Add one **very subtle** Purrfect Universe
  hint:
  - a tiny **paw-mark**, or
  - a minimal **whisker curve** hugging a corner. This detail must be
    **monochrome** and **barely visible** — like a watermark.

- **Accents (optional, very subtle):** Purrfect Universe tones:
  - `#d2691e` — warmth
  - `#228b22` — growth
  - `#1f77b4` — depth  
    Use sparingly; they must not overpower the blue.

- **Style Guidelines:**
  - Flat vector only
  - No shadows, bevels, glows, or gradients
  - Clean edges, slight asymmetry permitted
  - Keep the icon readable at small sizes

---

## Background (Chroma Key)

Render the design on a **pure solid background color**: `#00FF43`. This color is
**reserved** for transparency processing — do **not** use it anywhere else in
the emoji.

---

## Output Expectations

- Output a single **PNG** on `#00FF43` background.
- Keep the canvas square and centered.
- Leave slight padding around the shape.
