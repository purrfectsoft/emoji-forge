## 💡 Core Meaning & Emotion

`:tiktaalik:` is the **emoji of first steps**.

It honors the ancient moment when a creature said, “I will try what no one in my
lineage has tried,” and pulled itself from water onto land — not because it was
safe, but because it was _possible_. In the Purrfect Universe, this emoji is
used to celebrate:

- bold transitions (water → land, idea → product, private → published, closed →
  open),
- thinking outside the given box or habitat,
- honoring first-movers and bridge-builders,
- respecting evolutionary courage — _even when the outcome isn’t guaranteed_.

It’s the spiritual high-five to every “we’ve never done this before, but we will
now.”

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

- **Form:** Slightly tilted rhombus / irregular quad (tilt < 10°) to suggest
  _leaving alignment / leaving the usual medium_.
- **Base:** Single, confident base color from the accent palette.
- **Symbol / Mark:** A simple **evolution / transition** mark (e.g. a rising
  fin-to-limb glyph or upward change symbol) that can be read at 128×128.
- **Motif:** Water-to-land implication — bottom edge can hint water, upper edge
  land (abstract, flat).
- **Feline Signature (optional):** Tiny, integrated paw ripple or curve, to show
  that PU remembers its ancestors 🐾.
- **Accent Palette:**
  - `#f7df1e` (signal / achievement)
  - `#d2691e` (earth / land)
  - `#228b22` (life / growth)
  - `#1f77b4` (water / origin)
- **Background (Chroma Key):** Render on **solid `#00FF43`**. The pipeline
  converts this to **true transparency**.
- **Output Goal:** Discord-ready 128×128 transparent PNG + hi-res archival.
