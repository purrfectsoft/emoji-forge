# Gemini Generation Prompt — Purrfect Universe Emoji #007 (:tiktaalik:)

**Prompt Title:** Purrfect Universe Emoji – The Bridgewalker (Water → Land
Courage)

Create a **flat, vector emoji-style icon** representing **a courageous
evolutionary transition from water to land** in the **Purrfect Universe** visual
language.

This emoji honors Tiktaalik — the ancestor who carried the lineage from water to
land — and symbolizes bold transitions, pioneering acts, and thinking beyond
habitat.

---

## Concept & Mood

- Mood: **Curious, pioneering, brave.**
- Spirit: “I will go where we’ve never gone.”
- Should read clearly at 128×128.

---

## Visual Style

- **Overall Form:** A slightly irregular quadrilateral / softly tilted square
  (tilt under 10°) to suggest leaving the normal frame.
- **Base Color:** Choose a confident base from the palette below.
- **Core Symbol (very important):** A simple, bold transition mark — e.g. _lower
  water shape → upper land shape_, or _ascending limb/fin glyph_, or _upward
  evolution arrow_ — must be readable at small size.
- **Water–Land Hint:** Bottom area can discreetly reference water; top area can
  discreetly reference land. Keep it **flat and symbolic**, not illustrative.
- **Feline Accent (optional):** One tiny, integrated PU-style paw/ripple curve
  to show this is part of the Purrfect Universe canon.
- **Accents (optional, do not overuse):**
  - `#f7df1e` (signal / achievement)
  - `#d2691e` (earth / land)
  - `#228b22` (life / growth)
  - `#1f77b4` (water / origin)
- **Style Guidelines:**
  - Flat vector only
  - No shadows, no bevels, no glows
  - Clean edges, disciplined asymmetry
  - Centered composition with slight padding

---

## Background (Chroma Key)

Render the emoji **on a pure solid background of `#00FF43`**.  
**Do not** use `#00FF43` anywhere else in the design.  
Our pipeline will convert this chroma key to true transparency.

---

## Output Expectations

- Export **one PNG** on `#00FF43`
- Square canvas, centered
- High resolution so we can downscale to 128×128

---

## Notes for the Model

This emoji represents **evolutionary courage** in a cat-myth-driven software
universe. Avoid literal fish drawings; lean into **symbolic transition** and
**PU house style**.
